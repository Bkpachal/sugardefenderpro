How to read a dietary supplement label

Learn to distinguish serving size, active ingredients, amounts per serving, other ingredients, warnings and manufacturer information. A clear label makes it easier to compare products and ask informed questions.

Why ingredient studies do not prove a finished product works

Research findings depend on the studied ingredient, preparation, dose, population and outcome. A product that combines several ingredients needs evidence relevant to that exact formulation before product-level claims can be made confidently.

Questions to ask before buying a supplement

  • Can I see the complete current label?
  • Who is responsible for manufacturing and distribution?
  • What is the full checkout price?
  • Are there warnings or possible interactions?
  • What exactly does the refund policy require?

Blood glucose and everyday health

Blood glucose management is individual. Screening, diagnosis and treatment decisions belong with qualified healthcare professionals. General wellness articles cannot interpret personal test results or replace medical advice.
